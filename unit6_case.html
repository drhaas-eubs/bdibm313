Humane AI Pin: The Prototype That Skipped the Questions

How a 230-million-dollar AI wearable launched, flopped and was shut down within a year.

Humane Inc.United States2023–2025

Key Facts

  • Humane was founded by former Apple employees Imran Chaudhri and Bethany Bongiorno and raised more than USD 230 million before shutting down (Zeff, 2025).
  • The AI Pin launched in April 2024 at USD 699 plus a mandatory USD 24 monthly subscription (Zeff, 2025; Lazzaro, 2025).
  • Leading reviewers were highly critical; Marques Brownlee called it the worst product he had ever reviewed (Lazzaro, 2025).
  • According to internal data reported in August 2024, more AI Pins were returned than purchased between May and August 2024, and only about 7,000 units remained with customers (Clover, 2024).
  • Humane had originally planned to sell around 100,000 units in the first year (Clover, 2024).
  • In October 2024 the price was cut to USD 499 (Zeff, 2025).
  • On 18 February 2025 HP announced it would acquire Humane's Cosmos AI platform, more than 300 patents and patent applications and its engineering team for USD 116 million to form HP IQ (HP Inc., 2025).
  • AI Pins stopped connecting to Humane's servers on 28 February 2025, making the devices largely unusable (Zeff, 2025).

The Storyline (after Bostelaar)

Beginning · Misbelief

Humane's founders believed that a beautifully engineered, screen-free AI device would make the smartphone feel old-fashioned. The product was revealed on stage and in glossy demos before many ordinary users had tested it in daily life.

Middle · Insight

When real customers used the AI Pin, the problems were basic: slow and unreliable answers, a hard-to-read projector, heat and battery issues, and a subscription for tasks a phone already did. The core job, 'help me do something my phone cannot', was never clearly validated.

End · Understanding

A high-fidelity, expensive prototype answers questions about implementation, not about whether people want it. Cheap early tests of role and desirability with real users could have revealed the gap long before 230 million dollars were spent.

Your Task

In your group, use Houde and Hill's 'What do prototypes prototype?' triangle to decide which questions Humane should have tested first (role, look and feel, or implementation). Classify five AI Pin features with the Kano Model, using evidence from the reviews. Then design a low-cost Design Sprint plan (five days, under 500 euros) that would have tested the AI Pin's core job with 5 real users. Present your plan on one A3 sheet.

Discussion Questions

  1. Analyse: Which of the Product–Market Fit Pyramid layers failed first for the AI Pin, and how do you know?
  2. Evaluate: Was launching at USD 699 with a monthly subscription a reasonable way to find early adopters, or did it make Crossing the Chasm impossible?
  3. Create: Design a paper prototype test that could have revealed the AI Pin's main weakness in one afternoon. What would you ask users to do?
