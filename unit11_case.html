The Roundtable Dossier: Ten Ventures, One Pattern

A synthesis briefing of every case in the course — from quick commerce to AI labour supply chains.

Key Facts

  • Gorillas (Unit 1): Getir bought the Berlin quick-commerce start-up in December 2022 in a deal valuing it at $1.2 billion, down from about $3 billion in its previous funding round (Cantrill, Kandemir and Tan, 2022).
  • Mistral AI (Unit 2): about one month after being founded, the Paris start-up raised a €105 million ($113 million) seed round in June 2023, described as Europe’s largest seed round, before it had a product (Taylor, 2023).
  • Lovable (Unit 3): the Stockholm AI app builder raised a $200 million Series A led by Accel at a $1.8 billion valuation in July 2025, eight months after launch (Lovable, 2025).
  • Vinted (Unit 4): the Lithuanian second-hand marketplace reported 2023 revenue of €596.3 million (+61%) and its first net profit of €17.8 million, after a €20.4 million loss in 2022 (Vinted, 2024).
  • Langfuse (Unit 5): the Berlin-founded open-source LLM engineering platform from Y Combinator’s Winter 2023 batch raised a $4 million seed round from Lightspeed, La Famiglia and Y Combinator in November 2023 (Rawert, Klingen and Deichmann, 2023).
  • Humane (Unit 6): after raising more than $230 million, Humane sold its assets to HP for $116 million and switched off the AI Pin on 28 February 2025 (Zeff, 2025).
  • Jumia (Unit 7): in December 2023 Jumia closed its food-delivery business in seven African countries to focus resources on physical-goods e-commerce (Kene-Okafor, 2023).
  • Too Good To Go (Unit 8): by March 2023 the Copenhagen app had saved 200 million meals with over 75 million registered users in 17 countries (Briggs, 2023).
  • Builder.ai (Unit 9): in May 2025 the Microsoft-backed London start-up moved into insolvency after lender Viola Credit seized $37 million from its accounts, leaving about $5 million (Sun, 2025).
  • Sama/OpenAI (Unit 10): TIME reported in January 2023 that Kenyan data labellers working via Sama on OpenAI’s safety filters earned roughly $1.32–$2 per hour after tax (Perrigo, 2023).

The Storyline (after Bostelaar)

Beginning · Misbelief

Each case looks like its own story: a delivery war in Berlin, an AI lab in Paris, a fashion marketplace in Vilnius, a fraud-shaped collapse in London. Students often believe that success or failure depends on the one big idea, the technology or the amount of money raised.

Middle · Insight

Put the ten cases side by side and the same variables keep returning: how fast founders tested their assumptions, whether unit economics and truth kept pace with growth, and how well the venture fitted its institutional and social context. Money amplified whatever learning system was already there — good or bad.

End · Understanding

Digital entrepreneurship is less about a single brilliant move than about a learning loop that runs faster than the environment changes. The founders who won (or survived) tested, reflected and adapted; those who failed scaled a story before they had the evidence.

Your Task

Using the Roundtable Dossier, apply Cross-Case Synthesis to your two assigned cases: fill the five matrix columns and look for patterns that hold in at least three of the ten cases. Then turn your strongest pattern into a Toulmin Model of Argument claim card (claim, data from at least two cases, warrant, qualifier, rebuttal). Finally, present it in Pyramid Principle order — answer first — and state what this pattern means for your own Digital Venture Project.

Discussion Questions

  1. Analyse: Which enabling conditions (talent, capital, platforms, regulation, culture) appear in the successful cases but are missing or misused in the failed ones?
  2. Evaluate: Was growth speed an advantage or a risk across the ten cases? Weigh at least one success (e.g. Vinted, Lovable) against one failure (e.g. Gorillas, Builder.ai) to justify your verdict.
  3. Create: Write three ‘rules for digital founders’ that are supported by at least three cases each, and show how your Digital Venture Project will follow them.
