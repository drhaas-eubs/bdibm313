Course Units

Eleven teaching units following the AY26/27 course outline, in the EU Business School active-learning model. Each unit pairs a signature dynamic format — Presentation, Role Play, Mini-Hackathon, Business Challenge, Case Study, Prototype Sprint, Debate, World Café, Pitch Simulation, Ethics Tribunal or Roundtable — with a core message distilled in Kurt Bostelaar's method, its Intended Learning Outcome, a recent real-world case (2021 or later), a Thumbnail-Thinking framework gallery, pre-reading, a reflection prompt with key takeaways, and the framework authors. Expand a unit to begin.

1 Introduction to Entrepreneurship in Digitalization PRESENTATION ▾
Core Message · Unit 1

Going digital does not change what customers need; it changes who can serve them, how fast and at what cost.

Learning focus · ILOs 1 & 4 — Investigate how digital artifacts, platforms and infrastructure change the way a venture creates and delivers value, and communicate the main differences between a traditional and a digitally governed business.

Course Essentials

Intended Learning Outcomes

  1. Investigate the role digital technologies perform in improving traditional ways of doing business today.
  2. Critically assess the technical and social conditions which enable digital entrepreneurship to occur.
  3. Evaluate entrepreneurial management processes that involve a need for self-reflection and situational leadership.
  4. Communicate the primary differences between traditional ways of operating business in comparison with digitally governed business operations.
