BDIBM313 · Unit 7 · Reflection Prompt

Reflection & Your Personal Slibrary

Key takeaways and three questions for independent study

Sociological Considerations for Entrepreneurship in Digitalization · Format: Debate

Learning Focus · ILOs 2 & 3

Critically assess how culture, institutions, role models and support systems create the social conditions for digital entrepreneurship, and reflect on your own cognitive adaptability as a future founder.

Key Takeaways

  • Founders are shaped by context: culture, institutions and support systems decide who dares to start and what they can build.
  • Digital technology scales easily across borders, but regulation, norms and infrastructure do not — every market needs its own fit.
  • Cognitive adaptability is a trainable skill: notice, question and switch your strategy before the evidence forces you.
  • Role models and small successes raise entrepreneurial self-efficacy, which is often the real barrier to starting.
  • Good founders change their leadership style as the team and the venture mature.

Individually. In writing. No discussion.

Three Questions for Independent Study

  1. Jumia's leaders closed businesses and countries that did not fit their context. Which single decision do you think required the most cognitive adaptability, and why?
  2. Think of a moment when your own plan failed (a project, a job, a move abroad). Using the five dimensions of cognitive adaptability, describe how you noticed the problem, how you adjusted, and what leadership style you needed from others at that moment.
  3. Explain one framework from today's gallery in your own words (150–200 words) for your personal Slibrary: what it shows, how you would use it for your Digital Venture Project, and one limitation.

Question three becomes your independent-study task and the next entry in your personal Slibrary. Cite your sources in Harvard style.
