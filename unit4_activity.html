BDIBM313 · Unit 4 · Business Challenge

Business Challenge — The €100k Launch Room

Founding teams receive a seed budget in play money, choose a strategy, allocate resources and channels, and survive three market shocks.

Acting on Digital Entrepreneurial Forces · Format: Business Challenge

Learning Focus · ILOs 1 & 3

Investigate how digital ventures turn opportunities into strategy, budgets and channel choices, and evaluate the entrepreneurial management decisions and leadership needed to allocate scarce resources under pressure.

Opportunity is free; acting on it is not — a digital strategy is a decision about where to spend scarce resources first.

Warm-up · 10 min · Spend It or Save It

Every student receives ten paper tokens worth €1,000 each and three options on the board: hire a developer, buy Instagram ads, or keep cash. In 60 seconds, without talking, they place their tokens. The lecturer reveals the resulting spread and asks two students with opposite choices to defend them in 30 seconds each.

The Provocation

Vinted spent years losing money before it ever made a profit.

Every euro you spend on ads is a euro you cannot spend on product.

A strategy that does not say 'no' to anything is a wish list.

The best channel is the one your competitors have not found yet.

Read the lines with Vinted's 2022 loss and 2023 profit on screen. Ask each team to write down, before the challenge starts, the one thing they will refuse to spend money on.

How the Activity Runs

1
Form the founding team10 min

Teams of four or five receive a venture brief (for example, a second-hand marketplace for sports equipment in the DACH region) and assign roles: CEO, CFO, CMO, CTO and, if five, Head of Operations. Each role receives a private role card with one personal goal that may conflict with the others.

2
Opportunity and risk scan20 min

Teams build a TOWS matrix on flipchart paper and one Five Forces sketch for their market. They choose one growth direction using the Ansoff Matrix and write a one-sentence strategy that includes what they will not do.

3
Budget and resource allocation25 min

With €100,000 in play money (printed notes), teams allocate funds across people, technology, marketing channels and a cash reserve for 12 months. The CFO completes a simple budget sheet showing monthly burn, runway and a target CAC and LTV. At least three traction channels must be ranked with the Bullseye Framework.

4
Market shocks25 min

The lecturer releases three shock cards at five-minute intervals, for example 'Meta ad prices rise 40%', 'a competitor copies your feature' and 'an investor offers €50k for 20% equity'. Teams must react, rebalance the budget and record each decision and the reason on a decision log.

5
Investor panel20 min

Each team has 3 minutes to present its strategy, budget and channel mix to a panel of two students and the lecturer, followed by 1 minute of questions. The panel awards follow-on funding tokens based on clarity of choices, realistic unit economics and quality of decisions under pressure.

Deliverable. A photographed one-page launch plan with the TOWS matrix, chosen Ansoff strategy, budget split, Bullseye channel ranking and the decision log for the three shocks.
